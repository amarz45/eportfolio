## The short version

I build software that removes tedious work for other people, and I finish what I
start. On my co-op term that meant automation that turned hours of recurring
reporting into seconds. Outside of work it meant shipping
[Comicpress]({{< relref "/projects/comicpress" >}}) to Flathub as its sole
developer, from pipeline design through packaging.

## Why that matters

Most of what I know, I learned by building things end to end rather than by
being taught. That means I'm comfortable being handed an unfamiliar system and
working out how it fits together: at DataAnnotation I found and fixed bugs in
open-source codebases of 50+ files that had no existing issue or pull request,
which meant understanding someone else's architecture before touching it. It
also means I care whether the thing I built actually gets used, not whether it
was clever.
