---
title: "Values and Personality"
summary: "What I look for in work, including one thing the co-op term changed my mind about."
competencies: ["Professionalism", "Individual and Teamwork"]
---

Working through a values exercise for this course, two results surprised me.

The first is that I prefer working in an office. I had assumed I'd prefer
working from home, since nearly all of my communication with my team happens
online anyway. In practice the unplanned parts matter more than I expected –
lunches and hallway conversations with people from other teams, most of whom I'd
never otherwise talk to.

The second is how strongly I value difficult work. I'd have guessed I wanted
tasks to be comfortable. I'm noticeably more engaged when they aren't.

The rest is less surprising: I want work that runs over months rather than
one-offs, colleagues who are easy to work with, and honest feedback about
whether what I've built is any good. Outside work I care about keeping my
options open, which is most of why I don't own a car.
